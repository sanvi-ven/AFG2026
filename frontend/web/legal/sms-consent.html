SMS Text Message Program and Consent

This page describes the text message program operated by Ryan Prendergast, doing business as RP Landscaping, a landscaping and lawn care business serving Holden and Worcester, Massachusetts. It is published in plain HTML so that it can be read without JavaScript.

How consent is collected

Text messages are opt-in only. There are four places to opt in, each with its own unchecked-by-default, optional checkbox. None of them is required to create an account, submit a request, or use our services, and consent to receive text messages is never a condition of purchasing anything.

  • Clients — when creating an account in our scheduling app, Anchor, at https://anchor-orpin.vercel.app/signup/client: “I'd like to receive text message reminders about my appointments and invoices (optional). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.”
  • Employees — when creating an invite-only account at https://anchor-orpin.vercel.app/signup/employee: “I'd like to receive text messages about my shifts, job assignments, and my employment contract (optional). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.”
  • Prospective customers in the app — on the public work-request form at https://anchor-orpin.vercel.app/requests/new: “I'd like to receive text message updates about this request (optional). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.”
  • Prospective customers on our website — on the free-quote form at https://rplandscaping.org/#contact (also on each service page), directly below the phone field: “I'd like to receive text message updates about this request (optional). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.” Choosing “Text” as a preferred contact method on that form is not treated as consent.

There is no verbal, paper-form, keyword-text, QR-code, or purchased-list opt-in of any kind, and we never text a consumer number that was not opted in through one of these checkboxes. Separately, the business owner's own phone number receives internal alerts about new requests and job applications — an operational notification to the account holder about their own business, not a consumer opt-in.

What we send

Messages are transactional account and service notifications only — appointment reminders, reminders about invoices and estimates on your own account, shift and job updates for our employees, and status updates about a quote request you submitted (for example, “RP Landscaping: Thanks for your request! We received it and will follow up soon. Reply STOP to opt out.”). We send no marketing, promotional, or recurring campaign messages of any kind. Message frequency varies with your own scheduled work and account activity; there is no fixed send schedule.

Representative messages:

  • RP Landscaping: This is a reminder that your appointment is scheduled for 10/14/2026 at 9:00 AM. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • RP Landscaping: Invoice #12345 for $150.00 is due on 10/21/2026. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • RP Landscaping: Reminder — invoice #12345 for $150.00 is still outstanding. Questions? Call 774-559-9448. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to be removed immediately; you will receive one confirmation and nothing after that. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your mobile information is never shared

No mobile information — including your phone number and your consent to receive text messages — will be sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes, under any circumstances. Our messaging provider, Twilio, processes your number only as our service provider, solely to deliver the messages described above on our behalf. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.
