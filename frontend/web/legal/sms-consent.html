SMS Text Message Program and Consent

This page describes the text message program operated by Ryan Prendergast, doing business as RP Landscaping, a landscaping and lawn care business serving Holden and Worcester, Massachusetts. It is published in plain HTML so that it can be read without JavaScript.

How consent is collected

The only way anyone is enrolled in text messages is by creating a client account in our scheduling app, Anchor, at https://anchor-orpin.vercel.app/signup/client, and entering a mobile phone number. There is no verbal, paper-form, keyword-text, QR-code, or purchased-list opt-in of any kind, and we never message a number we did not collect through this form.

Directly below the phone number field, the sign-up form displays:

“By providing your phone number, you agree to receive appointment and invoice text reminders. Message and data rates may apply.”

Below that, the account cannot be created until the visitor checks a box that is unchecked by default and never pre-selected, reading “I agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.” Consent is a required, affirmative step, and a visitor is free to decline simply by not creating an account. Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of purchasing anything.

What we send

Messages are transactional account and service notifications only — appointment reminders, and reminders about invoices and estimates on your own account. We send no marketing, promotional, or recurring campaign messages of any kind. Message frequency varies with your own scheduled work and account activity; there is no fixed send schedule.

Representative messages:

  • RP Landscaping: This is a reminder that your appointment is scheduled for 10/14/2026 at 9:00 AM. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • RP Landscaping: Invoice #12345 for $150.00 is due on 10/21/2026. Reply STOP to opt out.
  • RP Landscaping: Reminder — invoice #12345 for $150.00 is still outstanding. Questions? Call 774-559-9448. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to be removed immediately; you will receive one confirmation and nothing after that. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your mobile information is never shared

No mobile information — including your phone number and your consent to receive text messages — will be sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes, under any circumstances. Our messaging provider, Twilio, processes your number only as our service provider, solely to deliver the messages described above on our behalf. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.
