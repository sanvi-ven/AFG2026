# RP Landscaping Fall Cleanup Policy 2026

## What's Included
Your estimate shows your package and price. Here is what each package means:

- **Essential:** one pass with the blowers over the main areas. Blow out beds to best ability. A little leaf litter in low-visibility beds and a few leaves will be left on the lawn.
- **Premium:** a full cleanup. Beds cleared (raked/vacuumed out), lawn and hard surfaces blown clean, final detail pass (catch last leaves on lawn / in beds), and a final mow to set your lawn back to the correct height to limit winter mold and help grass be healthy in the spring. Mowing includes mowing all grass, weed whacking all areas that the mower cannot get to, edging all areas to give your lawn the crisp look for the winter, and blowing off all hardscapes, leaves in street, etc.
- **Both:** two visits, so you are covered if the oaks drop late. The first visit (late October) will be everything in the essential package (keep your lawn looking good throughout the fall!), while the last service will be the premium and make sure the lawn is set for winter.

Your estimate is your price. If something on site looks different than expected, we will check with you first, and nothing gets added without your OK.

## Leaves and Disposal
We can do any of the following with the leaves once they are cleaned up:

- **Blow to woods or curb:** no extra charge, included in base price.
- **Bag and leave:** we bag the leaves and leave the bags on your property. Extra charge, shown on your estimate.
- **Full haul-away:** we take the leaves with us. Extra charge, shown on your estimate.

We only blow leaves where it is allowed. Some Worcester County towns do not allow blowing leaves into the street because of storm drains, so please tell us where leaves can go.

## Scheduling and Weather
- We give you a date window, not an exact time. The window can shift with the weather.
- We text you the day before with the plan for the day.
- If it rains, snows, or the leaves are soaked, we reschedule. There is no fee, but the date moves.
- If snow stays on the ground and does not melt, your cleanup moves to the spring. That is not ideal, but it is the safe call for your lawn and our equipment.

Weather, equipment trouble, or illness can delay the work. A delay is not a broken promise, and we will get you back on the schedule as soon as we can.

## Before We Arrive
Please take care of these before your service day:

- Move cars, toys, furniture, hoses, and decorations off the lawn and beds.
- Unlock gates.
- Pick up all dog waste.
- Mark irrigation heads, invisible fence lines, landscape lighting, and anything fragile in the beds. Alert us to anything that could be damaged while mowing/blowing leaves.
- Please keep yourself, kids, and pets away from the work area while we are working.

## Please Note
- We are not responsible for lawn damage, bare spots, or dead plants that were there before we arrived. We note these in our photos.
- If you think we caused damage, tell us right away or within 24 hours, with photos if you can.
- Our responsibility for any problem is limited to the amount you paid for that service, except where the law says otherwise.

## Payment, Rescheduling, and Canceling
- **Payment:** due when the work is finished. For the Both package, half will be due after each service.
- **Late payment:** if we have not been paid within 72 hours, a 10% late fee is added once. If you need more time, talk to us before we start work and we will do our best to accommodate.
- **Rescheduling:** let us know at least 7 days before your window and there is no charge.
- **Canceling:** if you cancel less than 7 days before your window, or we arrive and cannot do the work, there is a $50 fee.

## Photos and Referrals
- **Photos:** we take before and after photos at every job. This protects both of us if there is ever a question about damage. We may share them online unless you tell us to keep them internal. We keep sensitive details like house numbers, license plates, and people out of the shots.
- **Referrals:** if you refer someone and they book, we complete the service, and they pay, we will give you $20 as a gift card, cash, or credit toward landscaping work. Your pick.
